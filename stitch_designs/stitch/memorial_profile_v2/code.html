In Loving Memory

Eleanor was a beacon of gentle light in a world that often felt too loud. She spent her days tending to the quiet corners of her garden and the even quieter corners of people's hearts.

Born in the misty hills of Yorkshire, she carried the resilience of the moors with her wherever she went. Her laughter was soft, like wind chimes in a summer breeze, and her advice was always sparse but profound. She taught us that silence isn't empty; it's full of answers if you listen closely enough.

She is survived by her three children, five grandchildren, and a legacy of kindness that will outlive us all.
